ACL Verification — BLOCK-GUEST
Meridian-Router | Packet Tracer Lab
Author: Swayam Chopra | UMBC MIS 2026
=============================================================

--- ACL Configuration ---

ip access-list extended BLOCK-GUEST
 deny ip 192.168.50.0 0.0.0.255 192.168.40.0 0.0.0.255
 permit ip any any

Applied to: GigabitEthernet0/0/0.50 (Guest VLAN sub-interface) — inbound

--- Purpose ---

Blocks all traffic from Guest VLAN (192.168.50.0/24) destined
for the Server VLAN (192.168.40.0/24). All other traffic is
permitted. This prevents guest users from reaching internal
servers while retaining access to department VLANs.

--- Conclusion ---

ACL BLOCK-GUEST correctly enforces the security policy:
- Guest users CANNOT reach the Server VLAN (40)
- Guest users CAN reach department VLANs (Finance, Operations, IT-Admin)
- All other inter-VLAN traffic is unaffected
